About Us

Learn more about Leora Tech Solutions

Who We Are

Leora's Tech Solutions is a startup ICT and digital services company established in Arusha, Tanzania. We focus on providing integrated printing, design, and ICT solutions to individuals, businesses, and institutions.

Founded by Claytone Curthberth (Executive Director – Strategy & Vision) and Marco Jackson (Executive Director – Operations & Technology), we identified a growing gap in access to affordable, reliable, and locally supported ICT services.

We serve schools, hospitals, SMEs, NGOs, and government institutions with efficient, customized technology solutions tailored to your needs.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, reliable, and professional ICT services that empower businesses and institutions in Tanzania through innovative digital solutions.

Our Vision

To become the leading integrated ICT and digital services provider in Northern Tanzania, known for excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Story

Following market observation and assessment of ICT service gaps within Arusha and Northern Tanzania, the founders initiated the formation of Leora's Tech Solutions as a startup business operating under a phased growth model.

The business is designed to begin with high-demand, daily-use services such as printing, photocopying, scanning, internet services, and graphic design. These services provide immediate market entry, steady cash flow, and strong customer interaction.

As the company grows, Leora's Tech Solutions will expand into advanced ICT services including software development, website development, systems development, branding, and IT solutions.
